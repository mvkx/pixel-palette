01Start with a reference

PNG works best for pixel art. JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF work too, if your browser supports them.

02Keep it small, or keep it exact

Small palettes group similar shades. Exact mode lists every RGB color, ignoring fully transparent pixels.

03Make something of your own

Click a swatch to copy its HEX. Export a GIMP palette for compatible art tools, export a Paint.NET .txt palette (first 96 colors in the current sort order), or save a swatch image.
